# Notice Period and Exit Policy

## Resignation and notice

Employees who decide to resign should send a clear written notice to their manager and HR stating their intended last working day. The required notice period is defined in the employee’s signed employment agreement and may vary by role, level, location, or probation status. This handbook does not replace that agreement.

For most regular employees who have completed probation, the standard contractual notice is 30 calendar days. A different period shown in the employment agreement controls. Employees should not make commitments to a future employer until HR confirms the calculated last working day.

The company may, where lawful, agree to shorten or waive part of a notice period, place an employee on garden leave, or permit payment in lieu. These outcomes require written HR approval and are not automatic. Accrued leave cannot be used to reduce the notice period unless the manager and HR approve it in writing.

## Handover and company property

During notice, employees must continue normal duties, document active work, identify deadlines and risks, transfer access through approved methods, and support a reasonable handover. Passwords must never be shared. Managers should create a handover plan proportionate to the role.

All company property must be returned by the date instructed by IT or HR. This includes laptops, access cards, tokens, documents, payment cards, keys, and customer materials. Employees must not copy company or customer data for personal use. IT may disable access according to the exit plan before or on the final working day.

## Final pay and benefits

Final salary, approved expenses, statutory amounts, and any eligible leave payment are calculated under the employee’s contract, local law, and payroll schedule. Outstanding advances or lawful deductions may be applied. Because tax and legal requirements vary, the chatbot cannot calculate final settlement amounts or guarantee a payment date.

Health, insurance, and other benefits may stop on different dates. HR will provide location-specific information during offboarding. Employees should update personal contact and bank details in the approved HR system before access closes, not in chat.

## Company-initiated exits

Performance action, redundancy, misconduct review, and termination require appropriate HR-led processes. No manager or chatbot may promise a particular result. Employees with questions about an active case should contact their assigned HR representative and avoid sharing allegations or confidential documents in the assistant.

## Exit interview and records

HR may invite departing employees to an optional exit interview or survey. Honest feedback is welcome and will be shared on a need-to-know basis, though absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Employment verification requests should be sent to `peopleops@asterpeak.example`. The company will provide only information permitted by policy and law.

Former employees who need tax documents, benefit records, or a service letter should use the People Operations contact. The assistant does not retain documents, change a last working day, or issue an experience certificate.
