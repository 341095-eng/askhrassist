# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## General principles

AsterPeak Solutions reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary, and properly documented business expenses. Employees are expected to use good judgment and choose cost-effective options. An approved expense must have a clear business purpose and comply with both this policy and any stricter customer or department rule. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even when included on the same receipt as a business purchase.

Employees should obtain manager approval before committing to non-routine spending. Pre-approval is mandatory for air travel, lodging, conferences, training, software subscriptions, home-office equipment, and any single item above INR 10,000. Approval confirms business need but does not make an otherwise prohibited expense reimbursable.

## Claims and deadlines

Expense claims must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction or return from travel, whichever is later. Each claim must include an itemized receipt, transaction date, amount, currency, business purpose, project or cost center, and required approval. A card slip without item detail is not normally sufficient.

If a receipt is genuinely unavailable, the employee must attach a missing-receipt declaration and explain the reason. Repeated missing receipts may lead to rejection or review. Finance may return incomplete claims for correction. Approved claims are normally paid in the next available payroll or payment cycle, which may take up to 15 business days.

## Travel, meals, and transport

Employees should book travel through the approved booking channel. Economy class is required for flights under six hours. A higher class requires prior written approval under the applicable travel standard. Hotels should be safe, practical, and within the city guideline shown in the booking tool.

Business meals are reimbursable when they involve approved travel, customers, candidates, or a documented team event. The claim must identify attendees and purpose. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless a senior leader approved it in writing for a customer event. Traffic fines, minibar purchases, entertainment unrelated to business, companion travel, and personal upgrades are not reimbursable.

Public transport or a reasonably priced taxi may be used when practical. Employees using a personal vehicle for approved business travel may claim mileage rather than fuel, but this handbook intentionally does not state the mileage rate because Finance updates it separately. The current rate must be confirmed in the expense portal or with Finance before submitting a claim. Normal commuting between home and the assigned office is not reimbursable.

## Foreign currency and disputes

Foreign-currency claims should use the corporate-card amount or the exchange rate produced by the expense system. If neither is available, attach evidence of the rate charged. Employees should not calculate a favorable rate themselves.

Questions about a rejected claim should first go to the approving manager or Finance with the claim number. The HR Helpdesk Assistant cannot approve spending, alter a claim, or promise reimbursement. Suspected fraudulent claims may be reviewed under the code of conduct.
