# Hybrid and Work-from-Home Policy

## Standard work pattern

AsterPeak Solutions uses a hybrid work model for roles that can be performed remotely. Unless a department has a documented exception, eligible employees work from their assigned office at least two days each week and may work remotely for up to three days. Teams may designate anchor days for planning, customer work, or collaboration. Employees must follow their team’s anchor-day schedule even if another team uses different days.

Hybrid work is a working arrangement, not a contractual entitlement. The company may adjust an arrangement because of customer commitments, performance concerns, security requirements, training, or changes in business needs. Roles involving physical facilities, secure customer environments, or in-person service may require more office attendance.

## Availability and workplace standards

Employees working remotely must remain reachable during their team’s core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. local time, excluding approved breaks. Normal working hours, attendance rules, and meeting expectations still apply. Employees should keep calendars current and tell their manager about temporary connectivity or power issues.

The remote workspace should be safe, reasonably quiet, and suitable for confidential work. Company information must not be discussed where unauthorized people can hear it. Employees must use company-managed devices, approved communication tools, multi-factor authentication, and the corporate VPN when instructed. Devices must be locked when unattended and may not be shared with family members or friends.

## Requests and temporary changes

Regular hybrid arrangements are agreed with the manager based on role and team needs. A temporary change, such as working remotely on a scheduled office day, should be requested before the day whenever possible. Occasional emergencies may be reported as soon as practical. A manager may approve a schedule change but cannot waive information-security requirements.

Employees needing a workplace adjustment for a disability, pregnancy, or health condition should contact the confidential HR channel. The company will use the applicable accommodation process; employees do not need to disclose medical details to colleagues or to the chatbot.

## Location and travel

Remote work is ordinarily performed from the employee’s registered home location within the country of employment. Short-term work from another domestic location requires manager approval and confirmation that connectivity and confidentiality requirements can be met.

Working from another country may create tax, immigration, insurance, information-security, and customer-contract risks. The handbook does not define a universal number of days that may be worked internationally. Employees must obtain written HR, Legal, and Information Security approval before performing any work outside their country of employment. Approval is case-specific and should not be assumed from a manager’s informal message.

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides a laptop and approved software for eligible roles. Employees are responsible for ordinary home utilities and internet unless a written local benefit says otherwise. Any equipment purchase or coworking-space expense must be pre-approved and follow the expense reimbursement policy. Damage, loss, or suspected compromise of company equipment must be reported immediately to the IT service desk.
