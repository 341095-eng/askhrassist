# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

AsterPeak Solutions expects every employee, manager, officer, intern, and contractor to act honestly, respectfully, and lawfully. People must be treated fairly regardless of race, color, nationality, caste, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, marital or family status, veteran status, or any other status protected by law. Local law applies where it provides additional protection.

Bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats, retaliation, and deliberate humiliation are prohibited in offices, remote meetings, work travel, customer sites, social events, and work-related digital channels. A person does not need to be the direct target to report concerning conduct. Managers who receive a concern must escalate it through the appropriate channel and must not attempt an informal investigation themselves.

## Reporting a concern

Employees may report conduct concerns to their manager, any HR business partner, or the confidential Ethics and People line at `ethics@asterpeak.example`. If the concern involves the employee’s manager, they should use HR or the Ethics and People line directly. Emergencies or immediate threats should be reported to local emergency services and site security first.

Reports should include available facts, but employees should not delay reporting because they lack proof. The company will handle information as discreetly as reasonably possible; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed because a fair review may require sharing limited information. Retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.

The HR Helpdesk Assistant may explain reporting options, but employees should not enter names, medical information, allegations, or other sensitive case details in the chatbot. The assistant does not investigate, decide credibility, or make disciplinary recommendations.

## Conflicts, gifts, and integrity

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest, including outside work, financial interests, close personal relationships in a reporting line, or influence over a supplier connected to a friend or relative. Disclosure does not automatically mean the activity is prohibited; it allows the company to assess and manage the risk.

Cash gifts and cash equivalents are prohibited. Modest business courtesies may be accepted only when lawful, infrequent, transparent, and unlikely to influence a decision. Employees involved in procurement or public-sector work must follow stricter applicable rules and seek guidance before accepting anything of value.

Company records, time entries, expenses, and customer information must be accurate. Employees must protect confidential and personal data, use company systems responsibly, and report suspected security incidents promptly. Company assets may not be used for unlawful, deceptive, or abusive activity.

## Investigations and consequences

Employees are expected to cooperate honestly with authorized investigations and preserve relevant records. They must not conduct their own surveillance, pressure witnesses, or promise an outcome. Confirmed violations may result in action up to and including termination, subject to local law and a fair process. The specific outcome depends on facts and cannot be predicted by this handbook or the chatbot.
