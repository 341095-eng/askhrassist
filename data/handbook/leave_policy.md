# Leave Policy

## Purpose and eligibility

AsterPeak Solutions provides paid leave so employees can rest, recover from illness, and manage personal responsibilities. This policy applies to regular full-time employees in India after their date of joining. Part-time, temporary, intern, and contractor arrangements are governed by their individual agreements and are not defined in this handbook. Leave balances are credited at the start of each calendar year and prorated for employees who join during the year.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Employees should request planned leave in the HR portal at least five working days in advance. Requests for more than five consecutive working days should normally be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Approval depends on business coverage and is provided by the employee’s manager; submitting a request does not itself mean the leave is approved.

Employees may carry forward up to five unused annual-leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March or they expire. Annual leave cannot normally be cashed out while employed. Any payment due at separation is handled under applicable law and the employee’s contract.

## Sick leave

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical, preferably before the workday begins. A medical certificate may be requested when sick leave lasts more than two consecutive working days or where repeated short absences require review. Medical details should be sent only through the confidential HR channel, not posted in a team chat.

Sick leave is intended for the employee’s illness, recovery, or medical appointment. It does not carry forward and is not paid out.

## Casual leave

Full-time employees receive six days of casual leave per calendar year for urgent personal matters. Casual leave is normally taken in full-day or half-day units, cannot be carried forward, and is not paid out. Employees should notify their manager as early as possible. Casual leave longer than two consecutive working days may be treated as annual leave.

## Other leave and exceptions

Statutory maternity, paternity, adoption, and other protected leave are administered by HR under current law and the employee’s location. Employees should contact HR for an eligibility assessment rather than relying on a chatbot response. Unpaid leave may be considered after paid balances are exhausted, but requires manager and HR approval.

The company recognizes that bereavement requires compassion and flexibility. Employees should contact their manager or HR promptly so support can be arranged. This handbook intentionally does not specify a fixed number of paid bereavement days; HR reviews each situation individually.

## Recording and questions

Employees are responsible for checking balances and recording leave accurately. The leave figures shown by the HR Helpdesk Assistant are demo records and do not constitute approval. If a balance appears wrong, raise an HR ticket rather than changing the number yourself. Managers may not promise exceptions that conflict with law or a written employment agreement.
