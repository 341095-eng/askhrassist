# Employee Benefits

## Eligibility and enrollment

AsterPeak Solutions offers benefits intended to support employees’ health, financial security, and development. Unless a benefit states otherwise, regular full-time employees are eligible. Coverage and contributions may differ by work location because insurance and statutory requirements vary. The official plan document, insurer terms, employment agreement, and applicable law take precedence over this summary.

New employees should complete benefit enrollment within 30 calendar days of joining. Elections normally remain in effect for the plan year unless the employee has an eligible life event, such as marriage, birth, adoption, or loss of other coverage. Life-event changes should be submitted to HR within 30 days with the requested documentation. Employees should avoid sending medical records through chat or ordinary email.

## Health and wellbeing

Eligible employees receive company-sponsored group medical insurance. The company pays the employee-only base premium. Optional dependent coverage may require an employee contribution shown during enrollment. Deductibles, exclusions, hospital networks, waiting periods, and claim rules are controlled by the insurer’s current plan documents, not this handbook.

Employees and household members may use the confidential employee assistance program for short-term counseling and referral support. The provider does not give managers individual counseling details, subject to law and emergency exceptions. The company also provides an annual preventive health check where available.

Questions about whether a particular medicine, treatment, provider, or diagnosis is covered must go to the insurer or Benefits team. The chatbot must not give medical advice or promise insurance coverage.

## Retirement and insurance

The company participates in statutory retirement or provident-fund programs required in the employee’s location. Enrollment, contribution rates, and withdrawal rules follow law and the official scheme. Eligible employees also receive group term-life and accidental-death coverage at the level shown in the current benefits statement.

Beneficiary details should be reviewed after major life events. Beneficiary names and identity documents are sensitive and must be entered only in the approved benefits system.

## Learning and recognition

After probation, full-time employees may request up to INR 30,000 per calendar year for approved job-related courses, certifications, or professional memberships. Written manager approval is required before purchase, and reimbursement follows the expense policy. Approval depends on role relevance and budget; unused amounts do not carry forward.

The company may operate recognition awards and referral bonuses under separate program terms. A referral is not guaranteed to lead to an interview, and a bonus is payable only when all current program conditions are met.

## Boundaries and changes

Contractors, agency workers, interns, and part-time workers are not covered by this benefits summary; any benefits are defined in their specific agreement or applicable law. The company may amend benefit plans subject to legal and contractual requirements. Employees should use the HR ticket channel for an individual eligibility question. The chatbot provides general information only and cannot enroll a dependent, view a medical claim, or make a binding coverage decision.
